Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CalcHub by Mythbborrn ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our app or website. The App is developed and operated by Azubuike Christian Obiora (Mythbborrn Press) ("the Developer"). CalcHub is a simple, free set of calculator and converter tools, and we keep our use of data just as simple.

1. Developer Information

  • App name: CalcHub by Mythbborrn
  • Developer: Azubuike Christian Obiora (Mythbborrn Press)
  • Location: Delta State, Nigeria

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not require you to create an account, sign up, or log in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifiers directly.

All calculations you perform, including health, financial, math, physics, chemistry and simulation tools, are processed entirely on your own device. We do not transmit, store, or have access to any numbers, dates, or values you enter.

3. Advertising

The App does not currently display advertisements and does not currently include any advertising or analytics SDKs. We may add advertising in the future. Before we do, we will update this policy to name the ad provider and explain what data it collects, and we will ask for your consent again.

4. Device Permissions

The App does not request access to your camera, contacts, precise location, microphone, or storage. It only needs an internet connection to load its content.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user data with third parties, because we do not collect any.

6. Data Storage and Cross-Border Transfer

We do not store your personal information on our servers. The App loads its pages from a website hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). As with any website, the hosting provider may process technical data such as your IP address when your device requests a page, under its own privacy policy. This processing takes place outside the Chinese mainland. By using the App, and by accepting this policy when prompted, you consent to this.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 14 in the Chinese mainland). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

8. Data Security

Because we do not collect or store personal data on our servers, there is no such data for us to secure. Technical data handled by the hosting provider is governed by that provider's own security practices.

9. Your Rights and Choices

You may ask us at any time to confirm whether we hold any information about you, and to access, correct, or delete it. You may withdraw your consent to this policy by stopping use of and uninstalling the App. We will respond to requests within 15 days.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add new features or services. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date, and we will ask for your consent again if the changes are significant.

11. Contact Us
