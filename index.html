Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Universal Calculator Hub ("CalcHub", "the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our app or website. We built CalcHub to be a simple, free set of calculator and converter tools, and we've tried to keep our use of data just as simple.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

CalcHub does not require you to create an account, sign up, or log in. We do not collect your name, email address, or any personal identifiers directly.

All calculations you perform — including health, financial, math, physics, and chemistry calculators — are processed entirely on your own device. We do not transmit, store, or have access to any numbers, dates, or values you enter into any calculator.

2. Advertising (Google AdSense)

CalcHub displays advertisements through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers, and similar technologies to serve ads, including ads personalized based on your prior visits to this or other websites or apps.

Through AdSense, the following may apply:

  • Google may collect data such as your approximate location, device type, and browsing activity to deliver relevant ads.
  • You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You can review or adjust your ad personalization settings at adssettings.google.com.

3. Device Permissions

CalcHub does not request access to your camera, contacts, location, microphone, or storage. The app only needs an internet connection to load and to display ads.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade any user data. The only third party involved in CalcHub is Google AdSense, described above, which operates under its own privacy policy.

5. Children's Privacy

CalcHub is a general-audience utility app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

6. Data Security

Since CalcHub does not collect or store personal data on our servers, there is no personal data for us to secure. Any data handled by third-party services (such as AdSense) is governed by that provider's own security practices.

7. Your Choices

Because CalcHub does not create accounts or store personal data, there is no account data to access, export, or delete. To limit ad personalization, use the Google Ads Settings link above or your device's ad tracking controls.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add new features or services. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
